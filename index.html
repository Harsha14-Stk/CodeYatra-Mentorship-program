Three ways to mentor

  • Guest speaker: one hour a month
  • Squad mentor: about 3 hours a week for 8–10 students
  • Domain lead: sets standards for one track
  • Senior students review juniors' work, easing the load

What mentors receive

  • A modest honorarium
  • Industry Fellow title and certificate
  • First look at standout students for hiring
  • Recognition for mentees they refer and who get hired

Why companies partner, and how we keep it responsible.

  • Visibility as an industry partner
  • Leadership growth for your own engineers

Safeguards

  • Synthetic data only. No real student records in training

  • We promise skills and a verified portfolio, not guaranteed placement
